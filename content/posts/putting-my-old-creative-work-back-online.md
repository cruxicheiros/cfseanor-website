I took a lot of my old work offline when I restructured my website. I intended to put it back quickly, then months passed and I still hadn't done it.

In 2017-2020 I did a lot of creative coding. I slowed down with this and did more generally utility-focused stuff after that. It's something I'm interested in doing again if the idea strikes me. For now it's enough that it's still accessible.

Most of these sites work better on desktop than on mobile. I don't intend to change that any time soon: they're from a particular snapshot in time and I don't feel like it makes particular sense to go back and modernise them.

## Experiments
* [Algoscarf](/experiments/algoscarf) - Create a plan for an 'algorithmically generated' striped scarf, using sorting algorithms.
* [Mojibakery](/experiments/mojibakery) - Mash together or break apart unicode characters.
* [Tableflip](/experiments/tableflip) - Create your own custom tableflip emote.
* [Snek](/experiments/snek) - Poke a snake with your cursor. (Contains an image of a snake.)

## Games
* [Garbageman](/games/garbageman) - A 2020 game jam entry created with Ronan Hugtenburg.
* [Adsventure Game](/games/adsventure) - A "game" (play it twice to see why it's not _really_ a game) I made as a gift for some friends, about their dog.
