This is just a quick update to say that I've transferred the posts that used to exist at the blog Quiet Faith, Loud Opinions to this website.

(In fact, I will be setting the redirects to this website up shortly after I make this post.)

I've primarily done this because it was getting frustrating to feel the need to maintain multiple online personas and not really give any of them due attention. I also felt as though theming my blogs was stifling my ability to publish and write what I wanted to because my first question was "where will I put this?" which is a terrible question to be asking about personal writing.

The posts are backdated to the date they were originally published and can be found under the tag [#quiet-faith-loud-opinions](/tags/quiet-faith-loud-opinions). I've also re-tagged these posts to include the tag [#quakers](/tags/quakers) to indicate that they were initially written for that audience.
